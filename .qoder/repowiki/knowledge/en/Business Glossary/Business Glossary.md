### kỉ niệm
- Definition：Vietnamese for 'memories'; the folder name under which real couple photographs are stored for use in Chapter 04 of the A Little World For You website. In this project it refers specifically to the five night-time park photos that replace the placeholder slot marked 'Chủ dự án sẽ bổ sung ảnh sau'.
- Aliases：memories、memory photos

### A Little World For You
- Definition：Internal product name of a romantic apology/gift website built in Vietnamese. The design asset pack defines its watercolor/scrapbook style, six-chapter emotional arc, and two animated characters (a man and a woman).
- Aliases：Little World For You

### MASTER-PROMPT.md
- Definition：The single file in CODEX/ that contains the full prompt intended to be pasted into Codex to drive generation of the A Little World For You website. It is the source-of-truth document referenced by the asset-pack README.
- Aliases：master prompt、Codex prompt

### Codex
- Definition：The AI coding assistant (OpenAI Codex) invoked by copying MASTER-PROMPT.md into it; the design pack is consumed by opening the sibling Next.js repository in Codex and directing it to this asset folder.
- Aliases：OpenAI Codex

### concept board / moodboard
- Definition：The PNGs under ASSETS/ui-reference/ that illustrate overall style, characters, palette and layout for Codex. They are NOT production-ready transparent character layers and must not be used directly as website backgrounds.
- Aliases：ui-reference images
