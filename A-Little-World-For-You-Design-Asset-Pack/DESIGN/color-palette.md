# Color Palette

Primary background: warm ivory `#FFF9F2`
Paper surface: `#FFFDF9`
Blush: `#F7DCE2`
Rose: `#D98FA0`
Deep rose: `#8F4F60` (accessible accent; the softer `#A85F72` is ≈4.4:1 and for large text only)
Peach: `#F5C7AE`
Sky blue: `#DCEAF2`
Sage: `#C8D2BE`
Ink: `#59464A`
Muted: `#7A6466` (darkened from `#927D7D` for AA contrast)

Suggested balance:
- 55% cream / paper
- 20% blush / pale pink
- 10% peach
- 10% sky / sage
- 5% rose accents

Do not make every section pink. Alternate warm paper, pale sky, and botanical tones to create visual rhythm.
