# Story Overview

## Core message
“Anh không muốn chỉ xin lỗi để em hết giận. Anh muốn hiểu vì sao cách anh phản ứng đã khiến em buồn, và học cách yêu em bằng sự thấu hiểu nhiều hơn.”

## Emotional arc
1. Curiosity — a small world prepared for her.
2. Sincerity — a direct, personal apology.
3. Recognition — clearly acknowledge the two mistakes.
4. Warmth — remember the good parts of the relationship.
5. Commitment — three realistic behavior changes.
6. Gentle closure — affection without demanding forgiveness.

## Tone
- Natural Vietnamese, intimate, warm.
- Use “anh” and “em”.
- Avoid over-dramatic guilt, manipulation, blame, or pressure.
- Do not claim the website itself can fix the disagreement.
- Leave editable placeholders for personal names, dates, photos, and inside jokes.
